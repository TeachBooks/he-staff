# Safety committee

The HE Safety Committee focuses on prevention, awareness and departmental safety culture. It does not address individual cases, but looks at what we can structurally improve as a department. The committee strategically addresses all three dimensions of safety, namely physical, social and psychological safety. If you have any recommendations for enhancing departmental safety culture, please contact one of the HE Safety Committee members listed below.     

The HE Safety Committee is dedicated to promoting prevention, raising awareness and strengthening the department's safety culture. Rather than addressing individual cases, the committee focuses on identifying and implementing structural improvements that enhance safety across the department.

The committee takes a strategic approach to all three dimensions of safety: physical, social and psychological. If you have suggestions or recommendations for improving the department's safety culture, please contact one of the HE Safety Committee members listed below.
